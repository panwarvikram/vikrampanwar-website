Electronic Evidence & Cyber Law — Trial Practice Guide

A practical, courtroom-oriented study guide to electronic evidence under Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (successor to Section 65B): certificates, proving CCTV/email/CDR/WhatsApp/social media evidence, digital forensic chain of custody, illegally obtained evidence, e-discovery, and cross-examining the proponent of electronic evidence — with case law and a prosecution/defence checklist for each topic.

Based on Yuvraj P Narvankar, Electronic Evidence in the Courtroom: A Lawyer's Manual, 2nd edn (LexisNexis) — condensed and reorganised for courtroom use. For reference only; verify citations before relying on them in filings.
