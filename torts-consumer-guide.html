Law of Torts & Consumer Protection — Trial Practice Guide

A practical, courtroom-oriented study guide covering civil torts and Consumer Protection Act, 2019 practice: study notes, case law, a fast-lookup reference chart, and a plaintiff/defendant (or complainant/opposite party) checklist for each topic — built for district-court civil, MACT, and consumer-forum work.

Based on Avtar Singh & Harpreet Kaur, Introduction to the Law of Torts and Consumer Protection, 5th edn (LexisNexis) — condensed and reorganised for courtroom use. For reference only; verify citations before relying on them in filings.
