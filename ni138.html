Section 138 NI Act

Cheque dishonour — limitation & notice checker

Statutory timeline under Section 138 NI Act, 1881
1
30 days from date of bank intimation (return memo) to send legal notice — day of intimation excluded
2
15 days for drawer to make payment after receiving / refusing notice — day of receipt/refusal excluded
3
30 days from expiry of payment window to file complaint — cause of action arises on day 16
⚑ Use the date you received the bank's return memo — not the cheque date. Day of intimation is excluded from the 30-day notice period.
Must be within 30 days of bank intimation
Day of receipt or refusal is excluded from 15-day payment window
