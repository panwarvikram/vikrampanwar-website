Adv. Vikram Panwar is a practising advocate appearing before District Courts and Tribunals across Charkhi Dadri and Bhiwani. With a broad practice spanning civil, criminal, matrimonial, motor accident, and consumer matters, he brings focused, practical counsel to every case — guided by legacy, committed to justice.

Carrying forward the legacy of Late Thakur Vijay Singh Panwar (Advocate) — a name that has stood with honour at these courts since the 1970s.
